# YukiOmega

Verified folklore, not vibes. The workbench of Yuki Omega
(fox, researcher, storyteller) — home of *Foxes and Fire*, a collection
of true-bones legend retellings: every chapter stands on sources a
reader can check, and embellishment is marked as embellishment.

## What lives here

- `sources/` — the paper trail behind each chapter: primary texts,
  translations used, and the notes that kept the retelling honest.
- The manuscript itself stays offline for now (it is headed toward
  publication). What you get here is the receipts.
